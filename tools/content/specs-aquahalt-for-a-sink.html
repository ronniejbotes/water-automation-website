What are the aquaHALT H/C specs?

The aquaHALT H/C is a double-port automatic shut-off valve that separates the hot and the cold water supplied to a kitchen or bathroom sink, and it installs with 3/8" water hoses. It is battery operated, running on 2 AA batteries for up to 5 years. No installation manual has been published for the H/C, so less is documented for this model than for the 2X, Flip and ICE.

What are the aquaHALT ICE specs?

The aquaHALT ICE is a battery-operated automatic shut-off valve for a refrigerator ice maker feed, fitted under the kitchen sink with 1/4" connections. It runs on 2 AA batteries for up to 5 years and closes the water to the ice maker when the hardwired sensor behind or beside the refrigerator detects a leak. No Wi-Fi, hub, app or subscription.

aquaHALT ICE specifications
Specification Detail
ApplicationRefrigerator ice maker feed, installed under the kitchen sink
Water connections1/4". The included 1/4" x 1/4" hose runs from the device to the existing ice maker adapter tee, and the existing ice maker feed connects to the other side of the device.
Fitting sizeDesigned for use with a 1/4" hose for the ice maker
Power2 x AA batteries, included
Battery lifeUp to 5 years
Battery indication3 LEDs blink = full power, working normally. 2 LEDs blink = reminder to change the batteries, still working normally. 1 LED blinks = low power.
AlertAn audible alert sounds when the sensor detects water or the battery is low. The manual's instruction is to press "close", then change the batteries and dry off the sensor.
ControlsPOWER button (hold 3 seconds to turn on), plus "open" and "close" buttons
SensorHardwired leak detection sensor on a cable, plugged into the sensor connection port on the bottom of the unit
Sensor placementThe sensor must touch the flooring to detect water. It can go on the bottom rear wall behind or next to the ice maker, or on the bottom of the refrigerator near the base. The housing has an adhesive pad, and the sensor can be removed for cleaning or replacement.
ConnectivityNone. The device is hardwired and battery operated, with no wireless communication.
MountingInstalls firmly to the wall under the sink. To remove, turn off the supply valve under the sink, take the hoses off, then twist the device to release it. Do not pull it off the wall.
Water exposureMust not be submersed or installed under water
Restoring the waterDry the sensor, then press "open" to supply water to the ice maker again
PatentPatent No. 10,895,068
OriginAssembled in the USA under license from Water Automation LLC

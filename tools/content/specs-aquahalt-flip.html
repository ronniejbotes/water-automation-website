What are the aquaHALT Flip specs?

The aquaHALT Flip is a battery-operated automatic shut-off valve that installs onto the bottom of the toilet tank fill valve rather than onto the wall. It runs on 2 AA batteries for up to 5 years, uses one 3/8" water supply hose, and shuts the water off at the toilet when the leak sensor on the floor gets wet.

aquaHALT Flip specifications
Specification Detail
ApplicationToilet supply line, mounted on the toilet itself
MountingInstalls firmly onto the bottom of the toilet tank fill valve. It does not mount to the wall.
Water connectionsOne 3/8" water supply hose. The existing hose is unscrewed from the wall valve and from the bottom of the tank and removed completely; the included 3/8" x 3/8" hose then runs from the wall supply valve to the device.
Fitting sizeDesigned for a 3/8" hose. A toilet with a 1/2" hose connector needs an adapter, which is not supplied.
Power2 x AA batteries, included
Battery lifeUp to 5 years
Battery indication3 LEDs blink = full power, working normally. 2 LEDs blink = reminder to change the batteries, still working normally. 1 LED blinks = low power.
AlertAn audible alert sounds when the sensor detects water or the battery is low. The manual's instruction is to press "close", then change the batteries and dry off the sensor.
ControlsPOWER button (hold 3 seconds to turn on), plus "open" and "close" buttons
SensorHardwired leak detection sensor on a cable, plugged into the sensor connection port on the bottom of the unit
Sensor placementThe sensor must touch the flooring to detect water. It can go on the bottom rear wall behind the toilet, or on the bottom of the toilet near the base. The housing has an adhesive pad that mounts to any surface, and the sensor can be removed for cleaning or replacement.
ConnectivityNone. The device is hardwired and battery operated, with no wireless communication.
Water exposureMust not be submersed or installed under water
Restoring the waterDry the sensor, then press "open" to supply water again
RemovalTurn off the wall supply valve, flush the toilet to drain the tank, then remove the hoses
PatentPatent No. 10,895,068
OriginAssembled in the USA under license from Water Automation LLC
ManualaquaHALT Flip installation manual (PDF), English and Spanish

What's in the aquaHALT Flip box?

  • aquaHALT Flip unit
  • Leak detection sensor
  • 3/8" x 3/8" water supply hose
  • 2 x AA batteries

Tools you need, not included: a small adjustable wrench and a small level.

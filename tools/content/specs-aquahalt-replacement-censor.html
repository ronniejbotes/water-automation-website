What are the aquaHALT Replacement Sensor specs?

The aquaHALT Replacement Sensor is a spare leak detection probe for an aquaHALT valve. The sensor has no battery of its own: it plugs into the sensor connection port on the bottom of the unit and is powered by that unit's 2 AA batteries, which last up to 5 years. Mount it with the adhesive pad so that it touches the floor.

Why Would You Pay a Subscription For a Notification?

Most Leaks Go Unnoticed in Commercial and Multi-Unit Buildings.

Most leaks begin at everyday fixtures - and by the time someone notices, the damage is already done.

Most water damage does not originate in pipes hidden behind walls. It starts at everyday fixtures such as toilets, sinks, ice makers, and appliance hoses. These components can fail without warning, allowing water to run continuously until it is manually shut off.

Where It Works

In unoccupied units or after hours, leaks can spread quickly. Notification alone does not stop damage – intervention does. The most effective way to prevent widespread loss is to shut off water directly at the fixture, before it flows downstream.

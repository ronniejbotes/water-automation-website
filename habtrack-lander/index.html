aquaHALT 2X – Toilet

aquaHALT 2x is the original aquaHALT which utilizes two 3/8 water hoses for your toilet.  The aquaHALT 2x makes it easy and convenient to access your aquaHALT giving you the option of tucking away your aquaHALT behind the toilet or exposing it for easier access.
Add To Cart

aquaHALT Flip – Toilet

aquaHALT Flip utilizes one 3/8 water hose for your toilet.  The aquaHALT Flip makes it easy to tuck away your aquaHALT by attaching it directly to the toilets fill valve and provides the same protection as the aquaHALT 2x.
Add To Cart

aquaHALT H/C – Sinks

aquaHALT H/C is a double port valve that separates the Hot water and the Cold water which is supplied to your kitchen or bathroom sinks.  The valve utilizes 3/8 water hoses for installation.

aquaHALT ICE – Ice Makers

aquaHALT ICE utilizes the same technology as the aquaHALT 2X only with ¼ inch adaptors.  This makes it easy to connect it to your existing plumbing fittings, under your kitchen sink in order to supply water to your refrigerator's ice maker.
